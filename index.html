FAQ

  • Do I need an account? No. Inkelle works without any login; sync uses your own iCloud.
  • Do the AI features cost money or need a key? The AI study suite is optional and uses your own DeepSeek API key. Nothing is sent to DeepSeek until you enter a key and explicitly consent.
  • Where are my notes stored? On your device and, if enabled, in your private iCloud container. The developer cannot read them.
  • 需要登录吗？ 不需要。Inkelle 无需任何登录，同步使用你自己的 iCloud。
  • AI 功能要钱/要密钥吗？ AI 学习套件是可选的，使用你自己的 DeepSeek API Key；在你填入密钥并明确同意前，不会向 DeepSeek 发送任何内容。
